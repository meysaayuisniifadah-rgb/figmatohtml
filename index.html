Culinary Heritage

The Craft of Torch Ginger: Bondowoso Culinary Excellence

At PRIMA QOE, we elevate Bunga Kecombrang (Torch Ginger / Etlingera elatior)—an aromatic jewel that has graced Archipelago feasts for centuries. Its unmistakable floral-citrus vibrancy cuts through rich gravies, transforming traditional condiment making into fine gastronomy.

Every batch begins with hand-sheared blossoms harvested from fertile volcanic agroforestry plots in the highland valleys of Bondowoso, East Java. We slow-simmer the petals alongside sun-dried sea salt and refined cold-pressed oils. No MSG, no synthetic colorings, and no artificial thickeners. Only pure culinary craft, prepared in hygienic small quantities to preserve aromatic freshness at your table.
